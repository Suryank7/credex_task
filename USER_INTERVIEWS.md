# USER_INTERVIEWS.md — Customer Discovery Interviews

## Interview Script

**Goal:** Validate that engineering teams (a) have AI tool sprawl, (b) don't know if they're overpaying, and (c) would use a free audit tool.

**Duration:** 5–7 minutes per call.

### Questions

1. "What AI tools does your team currently pay for? (e.g., Cursor, Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, API access)"
2. "Roughly how much does your team spend per month on AI tools combined?"
3. "Do you know if every seat you're paying for is actually being used?"
4. "Have you ever audited or compared plans to see if you could save money?"
5. "If a free tool told you exactly how to save $200–$500/month on your AI stack — with specific plan recommendations — would you use it?"
6. "Would you share that report with your manager or CFO?"
